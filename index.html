About Me

I am a passionate Computer Science student from Lusaka, Zambia, with a strong interest in web development and front-end design. Throughout my academic journey, I have cultivated a solid foundation in programming, user interface design, and responsive web development, which allows me to create visually appealing and functional websites. I am particularly drawn to front-end development, where I enjoy translating creative ideas into interactive and user-friendly digital experiences. My goal is to continuously grow as a developer by learning new technologies, refining my design skills, and contributing to projects that have real-world impact. I aim to combine technical expertise with creative design to build websites that not only function seamlessly but also provide engaging experiences for users. What sets me apart is my dedication to both aesthetics and functionality, my curiosity to explore innovative solutions, and my ability to approach problems with a human-centered perspective. I thrive on challenges and take pride in delivering work that is both efficient and meaningful.
